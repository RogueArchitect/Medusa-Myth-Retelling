MEDUSA — Pitch Deck Outline (with your logline)
1. Title Page
MEDUSA  
Prestige Animated / Live‑Action Mythic Tragedy
Created by Mark

Logline:  
“Blinded to her own monstrous form, a cursed priestess wanders a world that fears her, unaware she is the terror they hunt — until a final, devastating encounter reveals the truth.”

2. Core Identity
A dark, emotionally charged reinterpretation of the Medusa myth told through:

psychological tragedy

divine symbolism

prestige‑fantasy visuals

adult‑oriented animation or stylised live‑action

Comparable tones:
Arcane, The Green Knight, Dune, Castlevania, Blue Eye Samurai.

3. Thematic Core
The film explores:

Perception vs reality — a monster who cannot see herself

Divine pride and mortal innocence

Violation, purity, and the cruelty of gods

Love as both salvation and destruction

The humanity of monsters and the monstrosity of power

These themes elevate the story beyond myth into prestige cinema.

4. World & Mythology
A grounded, psychologically coherent ancient Greece:

Gods behave with divine logic, not human morality

Temples, vows, and sacred purity matter

Monsters are metaphors made flesh

The world reacts to Medusa with fear, mythmaking, and military force

This section shows the world is cinematic, consistent, and emotionally resonant.

5. Character Profiles
Medusa — The Devoted Priestess
Loyal to Athena

Violated, cursed, and blind to her own transformation

Emotional heart of the film

Athena — The Wounded Goddess
Divine pride, sacred fury

Not evil — but not human

Learns through tragedy

Perseus — The Reluctant Hero
Compassionate, observant

Sees the woman inside the monster

Performs a mercy, not a conquest

The Lover — The Emotional Anchor
Represents the life Medusa could have had

His death breaks her illusion

His presence shapes the tragedy

6. Full Story Arc
A prestige‑fantasy 3‑act structure:

Act I: Devotion, violation, divine pride, the curse

Act II: Denial, accidental deaths, the cave of statues, the lover’s return, the shattering reveal

Act III: Perseus’s mercy, Athena’s reckoning, astral purification, the dual‑face shield

This is the spine of the film.

7. Key Scenes
The “trailer moments” that sell the movie:

Medusa’s shadow twisting on the temple wall

First accidental petrification

The cave filled with statues

The lover turning to stone in her arms

Perseus seeing the woman inside the monster

Astral purification

The shield revealing Medusa’s human face in the light

These are the iconic images that define the film.

8. Visual Style Guide
A prestige‑fantasy aesthetic:

Bronze, obsidian, moonlit blues

Serpentine motion language

Divine light vs mortal shadow

Reflections as emotional storytelling

The lamia form revealed only at emotional breaking points

This is where animation or VFX teams get excited.

9. Why This Story Now
Executives LOVE this section.

Medusa resonates today because it’s a story about:

trauma

perception

purity culture

victim‑blaming

divine systems that punish the innocent

It reframes a misunderstood myth without turning Medusa into a superhero — instead, she becomes a tragic, psychologically rich figure.
