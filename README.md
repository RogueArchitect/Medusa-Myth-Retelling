⭐ Story Summary
Medusa, a devoted priestess of Athena, chooses sacred purity over a budding love — a choice that becomes the emotional root of her tragedy. When Poseidon violates her inside Athena’s temple, the goddess’s fury manifests not as compassion but as divine punishment. Cursed into a monstrous form she cannot perceive, Medusa wanders the world believing she is cursed with misfortune, unaware she is the monster others fear.

Her denial shatters when the man she once loved finds her — and turns to stone in her arms.
This revelation sets the stage for her final encounter with Perseus, who sees the woman inside the monster and grants her a mercy killing. Athena, confronted with the consequences of her own pride, purifies Medusa’s spirit and forges her essence into a shield — a dual symbol of protection and remembrance.
